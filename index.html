Session rules

How every session gets booked, billed and paid.

  1. Every session gets a client name when it's booked.

    Book it in the booking app with the client's name on it before the session starts. No name, no session.

  2. No more clocking in and out for sessions.

    Sessions aren't tracked on the time clock anymore. The booking is the record of the session.

  3. You bill the session, and we bill the client.

    After each session, send the studio your session invoice naming the booking and the client. We send the client their invoice.

  4. Your pay for a session goes out after that client pays us.

    Once the client's payment clears, your pay for that session goes out.

Questions? Ask your manager or reply to the team email.
